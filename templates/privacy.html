Privacy Policy

At OneMarketX, we value trust and respect the privacy of every user. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, protected and managed when you access our website, create an account or use our services.

Important Overview

We are committed to maintaining a secure and professional environment for all users. Protecting user information is an important part of that commitment.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide clear and transparent information about our data practices. By using OneMarketX, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, you should discontinue use of the platform and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use OneMarketX, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide or that is generated through platform use.

This may include:

- Full name - Email address - Username - Password credentials (stored securely in encrypted form where applicable) - Country or region - Device/browser information - IP address or approximate location data - Transaction history - Support messages - Verification documents where required

We collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, secure and improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected information may be used for legitimate operational purposes including:

- Creating and managing user accounts - Processing deposits and withdrawals - Providing dashboard access and account history - Responding to support requests - Sending service-related notifications - Improving website functionality and user experience - Preventing fraud and unauthorized access - Meeting internal compliance or legal obligations - Personalizing language or regional preferences

We aim to use data responsibly and only for relevant business purposes.

3. Account Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user information against unauthorized access, misuse or loss.

Security methods may include password hashing, secure server configurations, access restrictions, monitoring systems and regular maintenance procedures.

No internet system can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their accounts by using strong passwords and maintaining confidential login credentials.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

OneMarketX may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website performance and user experience.

Cookies may help us:

- Remember login sessions - Store language preferences - Improve page speed and usability - Understand general traffic behavior - Maintain security sessions

Users may adjust browser settings to control cookies, though some features may function differently if cookies are disabled.

5. Payment & Transaction Information

When users initiate deposits or withdrawals, relevant transaction details may be recorded for operational, security and auditing purposes.

This may include payment wallet addresses, transaction IDs, timestamps, network references, amounts or account history.

We do not intentionally store more payment information than reasonably required to process or verify transactions.

6. Verification Documents (KYC)

Where identity verification is required, we may request documentation such as government-issued identification, proof of address or ownership confirmation for payment methods.

These checks help reduce fraud, unauthorized activity and account misuse.

Verification information is handled with confidentiality and retained only as reasonably necessary for security or compliance purposes.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

OneMarketX does not sell personal user data as a business model.

Information may only be shared in limited circumstances such as:

- With trusted service providers assisting operations - To process payments or technical services - To comply with lawful requests or legal obligations - To investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents - During business restructuring or operational transfer where permitted

Any such sharing is expected to be limited to legitimate purposes.

8. International Users

OneMarketX may be accessed globally. By using the platform, users understand that information may be processed or stored in locations different from their country of residence depending on hosting or service infrastructure.

We aim to apply consistent privacy-minded protections regardless of user location.

9. User Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable laws and region, users may request certain rights regarding their information, including:

- Access to stored account information - Correction of inaccurate details - Password changes - Communication preference updates - Account closure requests - Deletion requests where legally permissible

Some requests may require identity verification before processing.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate accounts, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with obligations or improve services.

When data is no longer reasonably required, we may delete, anonymize or archive it according to internal procedures.

11. Account Deletion Requests

Users may request account closure or deletion through official support channels.

Certain records may be retained where necessary for security, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, legal obligations or financial recordkeeping.

Deletion requests do not automatically erase historical records where retention is justified.

12. Children’s Privacy

OneMarketX is not intended for individuals below the legal age required to use such services in their jurisdiction.

We do not knowingly solicit or maintain accounts from ineligible minors. If discovered, appropriate steps may be taken to remove such accounts.

13. Third-Party Links & Services

Our website may contain links, integrations or references to third-party tools or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Users should review the privacy policies of any external services they choose to access.

14. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect platform changes, legal developments, operational improvements or enhanced security practices.

Updated versions become effective when posted unless otherwise stated.

Users are encouraged to review this page regularly.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding privacy, data use or account information, please contact our support team using official channels.

Support Email: support@onemarketx.com

Our Privacy Commitment

Trust is built through responsible actions. We treat user information with seriousness and continuously improve systems designed to protect privacy and platform integrity.

As OneMarketX grows, privacy and security will remain core priorities.
